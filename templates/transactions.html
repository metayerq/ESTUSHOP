What this page cannot tell you

Regulars — not measurable, and no proxy will do
The till is anonymous. Checked against the API: client.fiscal_id is '---------' on 59 of 60 tickets sampled since opening. Nothing here can separate a returning visitor from a first-time one, and no substitute (ticket size, weekday habits, hourly patterns) would be that measurement — it would only look like one.
People are estimated, never counted — and the estimate runs HIGH
Every figure on this page that says people is an estimate built from drinks: 1 drink = 1 person, floor 1, ceiling 8 per ticket. Two coffees for one person count as two people. So people / open day is an upper bound, and € / person is therefore a lower bound — the real spend per human is higher than the number shown. Tickets that hit the 8-person ceiling pull the other way and are flagged where they occur. The word here is always estimated, never customers; the till never counted a person.
June is excluded — and so is the opening
The analysis starts at analysis_start, not at opening_day. The café opened 27 May 2026; the weeks that follow carry the run-in and group orders of up to 55 drinks, which would move every median on this page. Excluding them is a judgement, and it is stated in the band at the top of the page rather than buried here. It also means this page cannot compare now against the opening period at all.
Holiday effect — no reference August exists
This is the café's first summer. There is no earlier August to compare against, so nothing on this page can tell an August dip from a decline, or a holiday lull from a lost habit. That comparison becomes possible in 2027, not before. Until then, treat every movement here as a measurement without a season attached.
No trend, on purpose
Roughly four weeks of open days sit behind this page. Nothing here is fitted, smoothed, projected or extrapolated: every line that looks like a line is a step of measured window medians, and every comparison is between two named windows with their n. A regression on this much data would draw the noise with the authority of a straight line.
