What this page cannot tell you

Regulars — not measurable, and no proxy will do
The till is anonymous. Checked against the API: client.fiscal_id is '---------' on 59 of 60 tickets sampled since opening. Nothing here can separate a returning customer from a new one, and no substitute (ticket size, weekday habits, hourly patterns) would be that measurement — it would only look like one.
Hours — not in the daily cache
Ticket times are not stored in the daily cache this page reads, so « are the mornings emptier? » cannot be answered here.
Holiday effect — no reference August exists
This is the café's first summer. There is no earlier August to compare against, so nothing on this page can tell an August dip from a decline. That comparison becomes possible in 2027, not before.
